9. Riding Passes

General:

In order to hire a Vehicle you will be required to select a Riding Pass which will determine how you pay for your journeys. Prices associated with each Riding Pass for your scheme can be found in the app in the “Wallet” tab.

You must also have a valid payment card on your account. Please refer to Section 11 for details on what is considered a valid payment card.

Where Minute Bundles or Period Passes are available for purchase, and when these have expired your balance will return to zero and your account will default to using the “Pay As You Ride” Riding Pass or the next level pass if you have purchased more than one pass.

For all Riding Passes, fractions of a Minute on hire will be rounded up to the next whole minute for charging purposes.
 

Riding Pass Options:

  • Pay As You Ride (PAYR). Following registration if you choose this product you will be charged a fee to unlock a Vehicle and incur a further per Minute cost for the duration of your hire.
     
  • Minute Bundles. Following registration, if you choose to purchase one of the available Minute Bundles (costs may vary), your account will be credited with the number of minutes purchased, which will be available for immediate use. Your minute balance will remain available until all the minutes have been used, unless otherwise stated in the specific terms of the bundle.
     

  • Pass and Bundle Validity. Please note that the validity period (how long the pass or bundle lasts) for passes and bundles may vary across different Beryl schemes. All passes and bundles activate immediately upon purchase. For specific validity periods, please check the Beryl app.
     

  • Period Passes. This type of Pass has an expiry date, this can range from a 1-Day Pass to an Annual Pass. There may also be restrictions on the journey time or number of journeys per specific period - please check the app for further details before purchase. Please note that Period Passes get activated once the purchase is complete.

Please note that not all Pass options are available in all Schemes. Riding Passes and Minute Bundles may also be scheme specific - please check the details in the app before purchase.
 

Pausing a ride: 

On all Riding Pass options, you can choose to pause your ride for a maximum of 30 minutes at a time. During this 30 minutes, the Vehicle is locked but reserved for your use until you resume your ride. The 30 minute pause period starts when you choose the option “Pause ride” in the app. Remaining time will be indicated by a countdown timer displayed in the app. 

You will be charged for the pause time in the following way:

Pay As You Ride (PAYR). Paused minutes will be charged at the same rate as riding minutes and will be added to your ride total. You will not be charged an unlock fee when you resume your ride.

Minute Bundles. Minutes on pause will be deducted from your total Minute Balance at the same rate as riding. You will not be charged an unlock fee to resume your ride. 

Period Passes. Minutes will be deducted from your pass as per pass specification, please check the app for further details. 

Other charges. When you lock your Vehicle to pause your ride, you will not incur any further charges during the 30 minute pause period. If the 30 minute period expires and you have not resumed the ride, your ride will be ended automatically and you will be liable for any charges due based on the location of your Vehicle. For details of these charges, see section 10.

 

Promotions and voucher codes:

At various times we may run promotions and provide voucher codes for discounted journeys or discounted passes. Promotions and voucher codes may be limited in their redemption and are subject to their own eligibility criteria. They are only redeemable via the Beryl Mobile App. We may modify or cancel these offers at any time. 11. Payment and payment methods

When you register, you will need to provide a valid credit or debit card, as listed in the Beryl Mobile App.

You must always have a valid payment card associated with your account if you wish to buy Riding Passes or hire a Vehicle.

To verify that you have a valid payment card associated with your account, we will run a pre-authorisation check on your payment card every time you attempt to hire a Vehicle. To pass this check, you must have at least £1 or $1 (depending on your Scheme) of funds available on the payment card. If using West Midlands Cycle Hire or Leeds City Bikes this check will be for £5. During this check, no funds are taken from your account. However, we request a temporary hold on the funds and while the funds are held they will not be available to you to use. 

Once the pre-authorisation check is complete, we will immediately cancel the request to hold funds. The funds will be released to you based on your bank’s procedure. We do not have control over and cannot accept responsibility for any delays in releasing the funds to you once we have cancelled the request. 

If we run the pre-authorisation check and cannot verify that you have sufficient funds available on your payment card, you will not be able to unlock a Vehicle. This pre-authorisation check applies to all users, including those with a Minute Bundle.

You may obtain a receipt for each of your journey records via the Beryl Mobile App and via email.

Fees for the Minute Bundles and Day Pass Riding Passes will be payable up-front in full. Pay As You Ride Riding Passes and any Charges will be automatically charged to your nominated credit or debit card at the time that they apply.

13.1 General Refund Policy

In certain circumstances, you may be eligible for refunds of fees paid for the Scheme. Each refund request will be evaluated on a case-by-case basis. To request a refund, please contact our customer service team (see "Getting in Touch with Us" section below).

 

13.2 Eligible Circumstances

Refunds may be considered in the following situations:

  • Service unavailability due to technical issues on our part
  • Incorrect charging of fees
  • Vehicle malfunction not caused by user misuse
  • Emergency situations preventing the completion of a journey

 

13.3 Non-Eligible Circumstances

Refunds will not be provided in the following situations:

  • Battery discharge during your journey resulting in loss of electric assist
  • Voluntary termination of rental outside a Beryl Bay or designated Zone
  • Additional fees incurred due to parking outside designated areas
  • Weather-related interruptions to your journey
  • User error or misuse of the vehicle
  • Violation of our terms of service
  • Continuing to use a vehicle after identifying an issue or malfunction
  • The phone running the app runs out of battery during the ride, preventing you from ending the journey

 

13.4 Battery-Related Issues

When using an E-Bike or Scooter, should the battery fully discharge during your journey and the electric assist stop, you will not be eligible for a refund for all or any part of the journey. Choosing to lock an E-Bike or Scooter out of a Beryl Bay or out of the Zone because the battery discharged is not considered grounds for a refund of any additional fees incurred.

 

13.5 Vehicle Issues During Journey

If you encounter any problems with a vehicle during your journey:

 

a) Immediate Action Required:

Should you encounter any issues that hinder your ability to continue using or securely locking the vehicle, you must:

  • End the journey at your nearest bay immediately
  • Contact our customer service team promptly (see "Getting in Touch with Us" section)
  • Failure to end the journey when experiencing issues may affect your refund eligibility

 

b) Safety Considerations:

If you notice any issues but can still safely use and securely lock the vehicle, you must document the problem for future reference. However, to ensure your safety and maintain refund eligibility, we recommend ending your journey in a safe location and contacting us immediately, as continuing to use the vehicle after identifying an issue may void your refund eligibility.



13.6 Manual Locking Procedure and Evidence Requirement

For all pedal and e-Bike models equipped with a manual lock, users are required to ensure that the vehicle is securely locked at the end of their journey. Beryl is unable to remotely lock manual locking mechanisms under any circumstances. In the event that you encounter an issue preventing you from locking the vehicle, you must provide a clear video recording demonstrating the locking issue. This video evidence must show both the lock mechanism and your attempt to secure the vehicle. The video must be submitted upon request by Beryl’s customer support team. If a video is provided at the point of request and it clearly demonstrates a locking issue, Beryl will manually end the journey on your behalf.

If you decline or fail to provide the requested video evidence, your journey will be ended by Beryl and a £10 unlocked bike fine will be applied in accordance with our standard operational policy. In such cases, Beryl will arrange for an on-street team inspection of the reported vehicle within 48 hours. During this inspection, a locking test will be conducted:

  • If the lock is found to be functioning correctly and can be secured, no refund will be issued.
  • If the on-street team confirms a genuine locking fault, a refund will be issued automatically for the affected journey.
  • If a reported issue with a bike is not inspected by our team within 48 hours of the report, any associated charges may be refunded to the customer at the company’s discretion.

This policy is in place to ensure the proper use and security of Beryl vehicles and to maintain the reliability of our fleet for all users.

 

13.7 Outstanding Balances and Refunds

  • Any outstanding balance on your account must be paid in full before a refund can be processed
  • This includes cases where you are eligible for a partial refund (such as first-time unlock fees)
  • The refund process will only begin after the outstanding balance has been settled

 

Example: If you have incurred a £10 unlock fee and are eligible for a partial refund, you must first pay the full £10 before we can process any refund amount

Failure to settle outstanding balances may result in:

  • Delayed refund processing
  • Suspension of account access
  • Forfeiture of refund eligibility

 

13.8 Processing of Refunds

  • Approved refunds will be processed within 5-10 business days
  • Refunds will be issued to the original payment method used for the rental
  • Partial refunds may be offered at our discretion based on the circumstances
  • All refund decisions are final

We may require supporting documentation to process your refund request. To support your refund claim:

  • Provide your username, date & time of the journey effected
  • Take photos or videos that clearly show the issue (e.g., malfunctioning locks, vehicle damage, or technical problems)
  • Document any error messages or app notifications
  • Keep screenshots of any relevant technical issues within the app

13.11 Theft

Reporting Stolen Vehicles

If a vehicle is stolen, it must be reported to the police immediately. Once Beryl has been informed, we will fully cooperate with police investigations. For updates on the case, please contact your local police department after filing the report. Any crime reference numbers should be submitted to our Customer Support Team.

User Responsibility

Users are responsible for the vehicle while it is on hire. If you leave the bike unattended, it must be securely locked. You must either end your journey or pause your ride. If pausing, do not leave the bike until the app confirms that the journey has been paused.Please be aware that if a vehicle is stolen while unlocked, the user will be liable for an unlocked vehicle fee.
 

13.12 Acceptable Use of Bikes

Our bikes are intended for active trips and must not be kept stationary and unlocked for extended periods. Users must lock the bike when not actively riding or moving with it, even if they remain nearby. You may not leave the bike unlocked and idle, including while sitting next to it at a cafe, pub, or other location. Doing so prevents others from using the service and contributes to poor availability. If a bike is left unlocked and stationary for an extended period, an unlocked bike fee of £10 may apply, even if the user is present.

To avoid charges:

  • Always lock the bike when not in active use.
  • Complete your trip if you intend to stop for more than the 30 minute pause allowance
